DREaM App - Account & Data Deletion

Account and Data Deletion Request

At DREaM, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal and medical information. If you wish to request the deletion of your account and all associated data, please follow the steps outlined below.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

To initiate an account deletion request, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our official support team at:
    mgmchms@mgmsopnm.edu.in

    Send Account Deletion Email
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - DREaM App".
  3. In the body of the email, please provide:
    • Your registered email address or user account identifier.
    • A statement requesting the deletion of your account and associated data.
  4. Once received, our team will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation email upon completion.

Types of Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data associated with your account will be permanently removed from our active database:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, contact numbers, and profile details.
  • Authentication Data: User account credentials, login tokens, and access logs.
  • Medical & Health Data: Medical records, history, assessment data, and any user-submitted health information.

Data Retention Policy

Certain data may be retained temporarily or as required by applicable laws:

  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Certain anonymized or aggregated transaction/audit records may be retained for up to 30 days or as required by law for legal compliance and reporting.
  • Backup Systems: Residual data stored in backup archives will be purged according to our standard backup lifecycle (within 30 days).
