Privacy Policy

At DREaM, we understand the importance of protecting the privacy and security of your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information.

Information Collection and Use

We collect the following information from you:

  1. Personal information: This includes information such as your name, email address, and other contact information.
  2. Medical information: This includes information related to your health, medical history, and other medical information you choose to provide.

We use this information for the following purposes:

  1. Providing and improving our services: We use the information you provide to deliver and improve our services, including conducting data analysis and research.
  2. Communication: We may use your information to communicate with you about our services, including sending you notifications and updates.
  3. Compliance with legal obligations: We may use your information as required by law, such as for reporting purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your personal and medical information for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and as required by law.

Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal and medical information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no system can be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by email or through a notice on our app.

Account & Data Deletion Request

If you would like to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal and medical data, please visit our Account Deletion Request Page or send an email to mgmchms@mgmsopnm.edu.in.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or the information we collect, please contact us at mgmchms@mgmsopnm.edu.in.

Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of January 1, 2025.
